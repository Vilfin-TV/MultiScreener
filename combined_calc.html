Wealth Breakdown

How your final corpus splits between the lumpsum's growth and the SIP's growth.

The lumpsum starts compounding on day one at full value, while the SIP builds up gradually — each monthly instalment gets less time to grow than the one before it.

Combining both gives you the lumpsum's full-horizon compounding and the SIP's built-in discipline and rupee-cost averaging, rather than choosing one over the other.
