Commodity Chaos: The Gold vs. Copper Divergence

You cannot understand this week's 20-point recovery without looking at the raw materials of the economy. Today, Risk Appetite was the entire explanation for the positive jump, and it was entirely driven by the metals market.

  • Gold (+0.22%): The ultimate fear gauge and inflation hedge is steadily climbing. Investors are hoarding gold as a direct response to the war impact and the erosion of purchasing power.
  • Copper (+0.55%): Often called \"Dr. Copper\" for its ability to diagnose the economy, copper flipped to a risk-on reading today. Why? Because the transition to \"megastructures\" and the AI infrastructure race (as detailed in our Nasdaq report) requires millions of miles of new copper wiring. It is the physical bottleneck of the digital revolution.
