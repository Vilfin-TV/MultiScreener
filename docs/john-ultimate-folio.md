# John Ultimate Simulation Folios

Prospective educational simulation, starting 26 October 2026. John is a resident
Indian manager with no other taxable income under the new tax regime. Separate
monthly funding of ₹20,000 for mutual funds and ₹20,000 for Indian ETFs; all
modelled entry charges are included. 84 monthly contributions, through September
2033. Normally ₹10,000 in each first/last trading-week window; October begins with
₹20,000 in the last week. Up to five distinct purchased schemes per product/month.
Whole monthly fund targets accommodate scheme minimums. Missing evidence defers
investment into cash; missed funding is caught up as cash, never as a retrospective
order using later fundamentals. Previously deferred cash can be reinvested in a
later month and is reported separately from new monthly funding.

## Selection and defensive alternatives

50 explicit eligibility gates, with asset-inapplicable gates marked N/A. Parking
funds have an additional credit-quality gate. MF Plan A targets 35% flexi cap, 25%
mid cap, 15% small cap and 25% multi asset. ETF Plan A targets 35% broad market,
25% Next 50, 25% mid cap and 15% eligible factor/small-cap satellite. Failed
allocations go to verified parking or cash. The algorithm may select fewer than
three funds when the evidence does not justify more.

Plan B reduces equity when the broad-market P/E reaches its 80th historical
percentile. Plan C uses verified parking/cash when valuation evidence is missing.
The last two years progressively reduce equity. The 16%+ annualised return target
is aspirational. No alternative is assumed to achieve it.

Look-through earnings hold the current disclosed equity quantities constant and
compare TTM earnings inferred from official NSE price/P/E observations a year
apart. This is not fund EPS or a forecast. At least 75% of disclosed equity,
including overseas equity in the denominator, needs comparable observations.
Unit-changing corporate actions are excluded. Proxy ETF holdings are insufficient.

## Execution, provenance and taxes

Source generation and private archive receipt must both precede the 14:45 IST MF
or 14:50 IST ETF decision cutoff. Market inputs must be no older than five days.
MFs require explicit fresh lump-sum eligibility, minimum amounts, applicable NAV,
stamp duty, truncated units and settlement. ETFs use the existing official
market-on-close rule, whole units, delivery charges and settlement. Cash reserved
for pending orders or unsettled redemptions cannot fund another order.

FIFO rotations require a verified cheaper same-category replacement, at least a
10-point ranking improvement, deterioration in earnings/valuation and estimated
immediate tax plus round-trip costs below 1%. Harvests are evaluated in March
using the shared MF/ETF tax profile, existing realised gains, long-term gain room,
actual costs and settled/load-free lots. Losses have no claimed benefit without
taxable gains to offset. Repurchases wait for settlement and fresh eligibility.
Resident annual income tax is estimated separately; it is not an imaginary
broker deduction. Rates are current model assumptions, not seven-year guarantees.

## Historical coverage and validation limits

1Y absolute return, 3/5/7Y CAGR, annualised daily volatility, maximum observed
drawdown and a 0–10 composite risk score use actual available daily data.
Since-inception returns require an observed start within seven days of recorded
launch. The current-mix history is a hypothetical fixed-unit buy-and-hold with
cash; it is separate from John's actual portfolio and SIP XIRR.

Historical monthly SIP illustrations include entry costs, but exclude investor
taxes and exit costs. They use today's selection and therefore have hindsight and
survivorship bias. They are **not** a validated point-in-time backtest of the
adaptive 50-gate strategy: historical disclosures and EPS snapshots for every
past decision do not exist. The website explicitly discloses this limitation.

The private hash-chain archives eligibility evidence and receipt timestamps.
The independent auditor recomputes selected gates, contribution dates/caps,
settlement, taxes, fees, cash, holdings, daily summaries and XIRR. CI stops
publication on audit errors. Historical transactions for the original 40
accounts remain unchanged.
