03Market Data & Analytics

Prices, index levels, fund data and other market information are obtained from exchanges, fund houses, publishers and other third-party sources. Data may be delayed, incomplete or revised, and is not guaranteed to be accurate or current.

Analytical outputs are produced by automated methods with known limitations, for example:

  • ETF Momentum Mode compares each ETF's weekly price with the chosen benchmark (RS-Ratio and RS-Momentum). The modes describe past relative performance and do not predict future returns. Thinly traded or recently listed ETFs may be shown as "Not classified".
  • Market Sentiment Score and screener ratings combine several indicators into a summary and can disagree with other methods.
  • Calculators use the assumptions you enter, such as return, tenure and inflation, and show illustrations, not promised outcomes.

Always verify important figures with the official source, such as NSE, BSE, AMFI or the fund house, before acting on them. Exchange names and data remain the property of their respective owners.

04Editorial & AI Content Policy

How content is produced. News summaries, featured stories, blog digests, briefings and some Academy material are composed with the help of automated tools, including AI-assisted writing, using information from publicly available sources.

Accuracy. Automated and AI-assisted content can contain errors, omissions or outdated information. It is provided "as is"; please verify important facts with the original source before relying on them, and report errors using the feedback form.

No professional advice. News, stories and briefings are for general information and are not financial, legal, medical or other professional advice.

Financial stories. Stock and mutual fund stories end with a risk disclaimer and may include referral links (see "Third-Party Links & Referral Disclosure" below).
