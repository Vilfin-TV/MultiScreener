02 · The Result

Wealth Breakdown

How much of your final value is your original principal, versus growth from compounding, versus what's lost to tax.

A lumpsum's maturity value splits into three parts: your principal (the amount you put in on day one), the gain the market added through compounding, and tax owed on that gain.

Because a lumpsum invests everything on day one, its growth compounds for the entire horizon — unlike a SIP, where later contributions have less time to grow. That's the core lumpsum-vs-SIP trade-off: full-horizon compounding vs. staged entry risk.
