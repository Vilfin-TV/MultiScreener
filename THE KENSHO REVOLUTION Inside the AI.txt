The Space & Skies Frontier: Leaving Earth Behind
The commercialization of orbit is no longer a billionaire’s vanity project; it is the backbone of modern global telecommunications and defense.

eMAXIS Neo Space Development (宇宙開発)
Trading near ¥62,600, this fund is the undisputed superstar of the Neo lineup, boasting a staggering +170.9% 1-year return leading into mid-2026. The fund captures the explosion of private aerospace contracts and satellite deployments. Rather than relying on legacy airlines, the Kensho index heavily weights new-space innovators and defense contractors. Major holdings driving this growth include Rocket Lab USA (a pioneer in dedicated small satellite launch), L3Harris Technologies, and legacy aerospace titan Lockheed Martin.
Future Outlook: As low-earth orbit (LEO) satellite constellations become the standard for global broadband and military surveillance, this fund will remain highly tethered to U.S. defense budgets and launch-cost reductions.

eMAXIS Neo Drones (ドローン)
While Space looks up, Drones look strictly at the lower atmosphere. Delivering a massive +96.2% 1-year return, this fund (trading around ¥47,500) has capitalized on the convergence of commercial logistics and modern warfare. Companies manufacturing autonomous aerial vehicles and the sensory software required to pilot them have seen aggressive re-ratings, anchored by companies heavily investing in uncrewed aerial vehicle (UAV) software and precision hardware.
Future Outlook: The drone market is transitioning from hardware to software. The future winners in this index won't necessarily be the companies building the plastic rotors, but the firms coding the swarm-intelligence algorithms that allow thousands of drones to operate in synchronized airspace.

The Energy Transition: The Subsidized Boom
The global energy transition is being fueled by trillions of dollars in government subsidies, making these sectors highly resilient to consumer market fluctuations.

eMAXIS Neo Clean Tech (クリーンテック)
Trading around ¥17,700, this fund captures the broader renewable infrastructure boom, posting a massive +186.0% 1-year return. Driven by heavyweight holdings in companies like SolarEdge Technologies, utility tracker Nextracker Inc., and solar-panel manufacturer JinkoSolar, it targets the hardware necessary for solar, wind, and geothermal generation. While it has seen slight short-term pullbacks in the past, its current long-term growth profile remains heavily subsidized by the U.S. Inflation Reduction Act (IRA).
Future Outlook: The narrative is shifting from "power generation" to "power storage." The Kensho AI is actively re-weighting this index toward utility-scale battery manufacturers and smart-grid infrastructure companies.

eMAXIS Neo Hydrogen Economy (水素エコノミー)
Sitting near ¥26,200, the Hydrogen Economy fund has been the ultimate hidden gem, delivering an astonishing +189.0% 1-year return. Tracking heavyweights like Bloom Energy, Ballard Power Systems, and cryogenic equipment maker Chart Industries, this fund is betting on hydrogen fuel cells for commercial shipping, aviation, and factory power grids—places where traditional batteries are simply too heavy.
Future Outlook: Highly volatile but fundamentally vital. As European and Asian nations mandate zero-emission commercial transport, hydrogen is the only mathematically viable solution for heavy freight.

The VILFIN TV Verdict: Strategy and The Currency Elephant
For Japanese retail investors utilizing their NISA Growth Investment Quota (成長投資枠), the eMAXIS Neo series offers unparalleled, institutional-grade access to global innovation. The Kensho AI's ability to read and react to corporate pivots ensures these funds never become bogged down by stagnant legacy companies.

However, VILFIN TV analysts must issue a critical warning: Currency Risk.

Every single fund in the eMAXIS Neo series is completely unhedged, and the underlying assets are overwhelmingly U.S.-listed equities. The massive 100%+ returns seen in funds like Space Development, AI Technology, and Hydrogen over the past year were heavily amplified by the historical weakness of the Japanese Yen against the U.S. Dollar.

If you are buying into the Neo series today, you are making two bets: one on the technological theme, and one on the exchange rate. If the Bank of Japan normalizes rates and the Yen aggressively strengthens, the JPY-denominated value of these funds will face a severe headwind—even if the underlying U.S. tech stocks are hitting all-time highs.

The Strategy: Do not chase yesterday's returns. Build a "Neo Barbell." Pair a high-growth software theme (like AI or VR) with a physical, heavily subsidized industrial theme (like Robotics or Clean Tech). By letting Kensho’s AI pick the winners inside those sectors, you can capture the explosive upside of the 21st century without anchoring your entire portfolio to a single narrative.
