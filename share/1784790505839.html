🇮🇳 Complete India Travel Guide — From Japan

July 23, 2026

🎬 The Big Picture

🔥 DRAMATIC REVEAL — From Tokyo to the Taj Mahal in just one flight! India is closer than you think — just 8 hours from Tokyo to Delhi. Whether you're craving the chaos of Delhi, the romance of the Taj, the beaches of Goa, or the backwaters of Kerala — India is waiting with open arms, spicy chai, and a billion smiles.

Welcome, Japanese travellers! 🇯🇵→🇮🇳 India is a land of contrasts — ancient temples and modern startups, peaceful yoga retreats and bustling spice markets. This guide covers everything: flights, visas, seasons, cities, food, transport, hotels, money, safety, itineraries and more.

🇯🇵 Tips for Japanese Travellers

🔸 Indians are warm to Japanese tourists — expect extra smiles!

🔸 Head wobble = 'I understand' or 'OK' — not disagreement.

🔸 If invited to someone's home, bring a small gift.

🔸 Traffic may seem chaotic — it has its own rhythm. Cross with locals.

🔸 English widely spoken in hotels, restaurants, tourist areas.

🛡️ Safety Tips for Japanese Travellers

CategoryTips
👛 GeneralIndia is generally safe. Keep valuables in hotel safe. Avoid walking alone late at night in deserted areas. Use Uber/Ola for taxis.
👩 Women SafetyDress modestly. Avoid alone after 10pm. Use women-only compartments on trains/metro. Carry a scarf to cover. Share live location.
🚘 Scams to AvoidTaxi overcharging: use Uber/Ola. Fake guides: hire govt-approved only. 'Free blessings' that demand donation. 'This shop is closed, come to my brother's' — decline.
💧 HealthONLY bottled water (sealed). No ice in street drinks. Avoid raw salads. Carry Imodium/Norflox.

📅 Sample Itineraries from Japan

🚀 5-Day Golden Triangle (Quick Trip)

Day 1: Arrive Delhi. Red Fort, India Gate, Chandni Chowk.

Day 2: Delhi — Qutub Minar, Lotus Temple. Evening train to Agra (Shatabdi, 2h).

Day 3: Sunrise at Taj Mahal 🌅. Agra Fort. Drive to Jaipur (5h).

Day 4: Jaipur — Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace.

Day 5: Jaipur→Delhi by flight (1h). Fly back to Japan.

Budget: ₹45K-₹70K (¥76K-¥1.2L) pp excl intl flights

🌸 7-Day Golden Triangle + Varanasi

Day 1-3: Delhi → Agra → Jaipur (same as above)

Day 4: Jaipur → Delhi (flight). Delhi → Varanasi (flight, 1.5h). Evening Ganga Aarti 🕯️

Day 5: Sunrise boat on Ganga. Kashi Vishwanath Temple. Explore ghats.

Day 6: Varanasi → Delhi. Evening shopping at Dilli Haat.

Day 7: Fly back to Japan.

Budget: ₹60K-₹90K (¥1L-¥1.5L)

🌿 10-Day India Explorer

Day 1-3: Delhi → Agra → Jaipur

Day 4-5: Jaipur → Udaipur (1h flight). Lake Palace, City Palace, boat ride.

Day 6-7: Udaipur → Mumbai (1.5h). Gateway of India, Marine Drive, Elephanta.

Day 8-9: Mumbai → Goa (1h). Beaches, seafood, Portuguese churches.

Day 10: Goa → Fly to Japan via Mumbai/Delhi.

Budget: ₹1L-₹1.5L (¥1.7L-¥2.5L)

🏆 14-Day Complete India

Days 1-7: Delhi → Agra → Jaipur → Udaipur → Mumbai → Goa

Days 8-10: Goa → Kerala (Cochin, 2h). Munnar tea, backwaters, houseboat.

Days 11-12: Kerala → Varanasi (via Delhi). Ganga experience.

Days 13-14: Varanasi → Delhi. Shopping, departure.

Budget: ₹1.5L-₹2.5L (¥2.5L-¥4.2L)
