🛒 Japan E-Commerce Titan Battle: Amazon vs Rakuten vs Qoo10 — Complete Foreigner Guide (2026)

July 25, 2026

Navigating Japan's e-commerce landscape as a foreigner can be a challenge. We have conducted a deep-dive comparison of the three giants—Amazon Japan, Rakuten, and Qoo10—to find out which offers the best value, speed, and accessibility.

🥇 Amazon Japan: The Expat Gold Standard

Amazon JP is the only platform with a 100% native English experience. Amazon Prime (¥600/mo or ¥5,900/yr) is highly recommended for foreigners.

  • Prime Benefits: Unlimited free shipping, Prime Video, Prime Music, and Prime Reading.
  • Shipping Points: Occasionally offers points for "No-Rush" shipping.
  • Product Range: Global brands, electronics, and books are best bought here.
  • Discount Deals: Prime Day and Black Friday are the annual highlights.

🥈 Rakuten: The Reward King (For Pros)

Rakuten is essentially a mall of thousands of independent shops. While the UI is chaotic and Japanese-only, the "Super Point Up" (SPU) system is unbeatable for long-term residents.

  • Discount Deals: Monthly "Shopping Marathon" and "Super Sales" where points multiply.
  • Shipping: Varies by shop (Look for Asuraku for next-day).
  • Range: Best for local Japanese food, furniture, and unique crafts.

🥉 Qoo10 Japan: The Budget & K-Beauty Hub

Owned by eBay, Qoo10 is the go-to for K-beauty and trendy fashion. It is strictly for those who can navigate Japanese or use auto-translate.

  • Discount Deals: "Mega Sale" (Megawari) happens 4 times a year with 20% coupons.
  • Points: "Mameballs" and "MameQ" can be collected for extra discounts.
  • Shipping: Slower as many items ship from Korea.

🛡️ Shipping & Points Analysis

Amazon wins on Shipping Speed (Same-day delivery in major cities). Rakuten wins on Points Value, often allowing you to pay your phone bill or credit card with points. Qoo10 wins on Direct Coupons which slash prices instantly at checkout.

🎯 Final Conclusion

If you are new to Japan: Start with Amazon. If you've been here 2+ years and have a Rakuten Credit Card: Switch to Rakuten for bulk buys. If you love Korean skincare: Qoo10 is your destination.
