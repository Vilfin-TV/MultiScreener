Japanese powerhouse artist CHANMINA is back with a blazing new single — "Firework". Known for her genre-defying blend of rap, pop, and raw emotional delivery, CHANMINA once again proves why she is one of the most compelling voices in the Japanese music scene today.

🔥 About "Firework"

Firework is a high-energy anthem that captures the spirit of self-expression and resilience — themes that have always defined CHANMINA's artistry. The track layers driving beats with her signature mix of melodic verses and sharp rap lines, building to an explosive chorus that is impossible to ignore.

The music video is a visual spectacle — fast cuts, vivid colour palettes, and choreography that matches the song's kinetic energy frame by frame. It is the kind of video that rewards repeat viewing, revealing new details each time.

👩‍🎤 Who is CHANMINA?

Born in 2000 to a Japanese mother and Korean father, CHANMINA made her debut as a teenager and quickly built a fanbase that transcended the usual J-pop boundaries. She writes and produces much of her own material, rapping in both Japanese and Korean across her catalogue.

International audiences first discovered her through the Chainsaw Man anime ending theme "OVER THE TOP" — one of the most talked-about anime songs of 2022. She has since released a string of singles and albums that consistently chart in Japan, with a growing global following on streaming platforms.

🎬 The Artist Behind the Music

What sets CHANMINA apart is her unapologetic creative control. She has spoken openly about navigating the Japanese music industry as a mixed-race artist, using her music as both personal expression and cultural commentary. Firework feels like the next chapter of that journey — bigger, brighter, and more confident than ever.
