Indias Aviation Takeoff: How Domestic Airports Are Becoming Global Hubs

NEW DELHI — On a sweltering June morning, a British Airways Boeing 787 touches down at Indira Gandhi International Airport Terminal 3. The arrivals hall buzzes with Hindi, Tamil, Mandarin, Arabic, English. This is no longer just Indias gateway to the world. It is rapidly becoming the worlds gateway to India.

The Mega-Projects Jewar

Designed to handle 70 million passengers annually across four phases, Jewars Phase 1A became operational on April 17, 2025. The Yamuna Expressway provides a 90-minute link from central Delhi.

Navi Mumbai International Airport

Construction on the ₹16,700 crore project began in 2018. Terminal 1 is targeted for late 2025. Full completion in 2032 will handle 60 million passengers across three terminals.

Southern Expansion

Bengaluru T2 opened January 2023 doubling capacity to 50 million. Hyderabad handles 40 percent of Indias pharmaceutical exports by air. Cochin is the worlds first fully solar-powered airport connecting Kerala to 35 global destinations.

UDAN Revolution

Over 70 dormant airports revived. 500 plus UDAN routes operational. 160 million plus domestic passengers annually making India the third largest domestic aviation market globally.

New Immigration Laws and Transit Facility

India introduced new immigration rules in 2025 allowing international passengers connecting to domestic flights to transfer without clearing immigration. The ITD Transit Facility is operational at five major airports powered by Digi Yatra biometric verification.

Rail and Road Connectivity

Delhi Airport Express connects New Delhi Railway Station to IGI Terminal 3 in under 20 minutes at 160 km per hour. Mumbai Metro Line 3 connects CSMIA to the city center. Bengaluru Metro extension is under construction for 2026-27.

Cargo and Logistics

Ten priority cargo hubs identified. Target of 10 million tonnes by 2030. The upcoming Multi-Modal Cargo Hub at Jewar will be Indias largest integrating air rail and road freight.

Phase Wise Roadmap

Phase 1 2024-2026: Foundation with Jewar Phase 1 Navi Mumbai Terminal 1 Delhi T3 expansion and over 500 UDAN routes. Phase 2 2027-2029: Scale-Up with Navi Mumbai 60 million capacity Delhi T3 90 million and Hyderabad Metro airport. Phase 3 2030-2032: Maturity with 140 airports 350 million passengers and 10 million tonnes cargo. Phase 4 2033-2035: Global Hub with over 1 billion passengers annually.

Conclusion

Indias airport transformation connects 1.4 billion people to the global economy. The challenges are real including land acquisition environmental clearances and workforce shortages. But the trajectory is unmistakable. India is building one of the worlds great aviation crossroads.

The sky is no longer the limit for India. It is the beginning of a new era where India does not just participate in global aviation it leads it. — Minister of Civil Aviation India Aviation Summit 2025
