October 16, 2026

Updated October 16, 2026 — comprehensive, cross-checked guide covering last month's OTT highlights, October's live and upcoming drops, theatrical blockbusters heading to your screen, and the biggest streaming event of 2026 on the horizon.

Three weeks into October and the streaming landscape has rarely felt this charged. Drishyam The Conclusion just crossed ₹507 crore globally in only two weeks — Vijay Salgaonkar's franchise farewell is already the second highest-grossing Indian film of 2026, and the clock is ticking before Ajay Devgn's most beloved character arrives on your sofa. Jailer 2 released yesterday with Rajinikanth as Tiger Muthuvel Pandian, Vidya Balan in a fierce new avatar, and Hrithik Roshan in a cameo that has Reddit in chaos — that one will land on JioHotstar within six weeks. Meanwhile, Netflix's October OTT premiere is a Neeraj Pandey crime thriller built around Manoj Bajpayee playing a bent Delhi cop on the most chaotic night of his career, and it arrives in exactly one week. And hovering over all of it, just three weeks away in cinemas: Ramayana – Part: I — Ranbir Kapoor as Ram, Yash as Ravana, Sai Pallavi as Sita, Hans Zimmer and A.R. Rahman sharing a score, and Sunny Deol as Hanuman waiting at the end of the film like Baahubali's "Why Kattappa killed Baahubali?" moment. India's biggest theatrical gamble of the decade lands November 5. Here is everything — September's OTT slate you may have missed, October's confirmed drops, and the blockbuster calendar through the end of the year.

October's Big OTT Premiere — Gajab Raat (Netflix, Oct 23)

Gajab Raat — Netflix | Oct 23 | Hindi Crime Thriller

Director: Neeraj Pandey  |  Cast: Manoj Bajpayee, Divya Dutta, Nushrratt Bharuccha, Saqib Saleem, Akshay Oberoi, Kiku Sharda

Inspector Ajay Dikshit — a corrupt, money-hungry Delhi cop with plans for a very profitable night — has his scheme spectacularly derailed when a badly injured girl is thrown from a moving car in the heart of the city. What begins as one inconvenient case spirals into a global conspiracy that Dikshit never wanted and cannot walk away from. Neeraj Pandey (Special 26, Baby, A Wednesday) returns to Netflix after the film's original title controversy forced a rename. Manoj Bajpayee plays his most morally ambiguous role in years — not the noble cop but the pragmatic one suddenly in far over his head. Trailer reaction has been overwhelmingly positive.

October 23 OTT Drop — Sumo Didi (ZEE5)

Sumo Didi — ZEE5 | Oct 23 | Hindi Sports Drama

Director: Jayant Rohatgi  |  Cast: Shriyam Bhagnani, Anubha Fatehpura, Nitesh Pandey, Chaitanya Sharma

Inspired by the true story of Hetal Dave — India's pioneering female sumo wrestler who broke every barrier the sport could place in her path. A sports drama with a completely uncharted subject for Indian cinema, and that novelty alone makes it worth a watch when it lands on October 23.

Malayalam OTT — September & October 2026 Complete Guide

⭐ This Week's Malayalam Frontrunners — Don't Miss These Two

Antappante Athbudha Pravarthikal — Prime Video | Oct 3 | Fantasy Comedy | IMDb 8.6

The week's biggest surprise and September-October's most acclaimed Malayalam OTT original. An ordinary man's mundane life starts producing inexplicable, extraordinary events around him — and nobody can explain why. The title translates as "Antappan's Wondrous Adventures," and an 8.6 on IMDb tells you audiences are responding to exactly the kind of warm, inventive storytelling Malayalam cinema does better than almost anyone. No spectacle required — just a sharp script and an actor who knows how to anchor absurdity with sincerity. Watch this before the discourse catches up.

Bethlehem Kudumba Unit — JioHotstar | Oct 2 | Family Drama | IMDb 8.1 | #1 Trending India

Two Malayalam originals in a single week — rare, and both worth your time. Bethlehem Kudumba Unit is a family drama rooted in Christian community life in central Kerala, following an extended family through a single significant event that brings long-suppressed stories to the surface. At 8.1 and trending #1 on JioHotstar India, this is clearly landing with exactly the people it is made for.

Jailer 2 — Full Story

Jailer 2 — Tamil + Hindi | Dir: Nelson Dilipkumar | Sun Pictures | Released Oct 15

Full Cast: Rajinikanth, Ramya Krishnan, Vidya Balan, SJ Suryah, Shiva Rajkumar, Mohanlal, Jackie Shroff, Suraj Venjaramoodu, Hrithik Roshan (special appearance) | Music: Anirudh Ravichander

After Tiger Muthuvel Pandian eliminates the Varman syndicate in Part 1, he relocates his grieving family to remote Tamil Nadu and lives as a quiet supermarket supervisor. But Varman's close aide Dileep (Suraj Venjaramoodu) is hunting for Varman's killer and three truckloads of hidden ancient statues. A mundane clash with a local fruit shop owner inadvertently leads Dileep's men straight to Tiger. Nelson Dilipkumar goes bigger in every dimension — the film ends on Hrithik Roshan's entry that explicitly promises Jailer 3. This is Rajinikanth at 75 doing things that will make you forget about age entirely.

Rated U/A 16+ | 14.15 lakh advance tickets on Day 1 | Vidya Balan in fierce villain mode

Drishyam The Conclusion — Full Story

Drishyam The Conclusion — Hindi | Oct 2 | Dir: Abhishek Pathak

Cast: Ajay Devgn, Shriya Saran, Ishita Dutta, Tabu

Years after covering up the accidental death that launched the entire franchise, Vijay Salgaonkar's family faces a new adversary — someone close enough to the original truth to destroy everything Vijay has protected. Mohanlal, who played Vijay in the original Malayalam Drishyam, publicly praised the conclusion. Makers have confirmed there will be no Drishyam 4 — a rare Indian franchise that ends by deliberate choice.

₹507 crore worldwide | Second highest-grossing Indian film of 2026 | ₹240 crore opening week

Prahaar — The Ujjwal Nikam Story — Full Story

Prahaar — Hindi | Oct 16 | Dir: Avinash Arun

Cast: Rajkummar Rao as Ujjwal Nikam, Lalit Prabhakar as Ajmal Kasab, Ritvik Sahore in a pivotal role

A courtroom legal drama built on the career of Ujjwal Nikam — the Special Public Prosecutor who secured the death sentence against Ajmal Kasab for the 26/11 Mumbai attacks. Rajkummar Rao delivers what early critics are calling the performance of his career. Released today — early audience reaction is very strong.

The Scale

The casting of Arun Govil — who played Lord Ram in the Doordarshan Ramayana that an entire generation grew up watching — as King Dashrath is a masterstroke of narrative poetry. The man who was Ram for India now plays Ram's father, passing the mantle on screen. Whether Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana soars or stumbles, nothing else in Indian cinema this decade will be as widely discussed.
