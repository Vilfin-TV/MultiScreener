OTT Update: September's Best Picks Still Streaming + October Arrivals + What's Coming in November 2026

October 16, 2026

Updated October 16, 2026 — comprehensive cross-checked guide covering last month's OTT highlights, October's live and upcoming drops, theatrical blockbusters heading to your screen, and the biggest streaming event of 2026 on the horizon.

Three weeks into October and the streaming landscape has rarely felt this charged. Drishyam The Conclusion just crossed ₹507 crore globally in only two weeks — Vijay Salgaonkar's franchise farewell is already the second highest-grossing Indian film of 2026, and the clock is ticking before Ajay Devgn's most beloved character arrives on your sofa. Jailer 2 released yesterday with Rajinikanth as Tiger Muthuvel Pandian, Vidya Balan in a fierce new avatar, and Hrithik Roshan in a cameo that has Reddit in chaos — that one will land on JioHotstar within six weeks. Meanwhile, Netflix's October OTT premiere is a Neeraj Pandey crime thriller built around Manoj Bajpayee playing a bent Delhi cop on the most chaotic night of his career, and it arrives in exactly one week. And hovering over all of it, just three weeks away in cinemas: Ramayana – Part: I — Ranbir Kapoor as Ram, Yash as Ravana, Sai Pallavi as Sita, Hans Zimmer and A.R. Rahman sharing a score, and Sunny Deol as Hanuman waiting at the end of the film like Baahubali's "Why Kattappa killed Baahubali?" moment. India's biggest theatrical gamble of the decade lands November 5. Here is everything — September's OTT slate you may have missed, October's confirmed drops, and the blockbuster calendar through the end of the year.

Malayalam OTT — October 2026 Complete Guide

⭐ This Week's Malayalam Highlights (Oct 15–16)

Aasha — ZEE5 | Oct 16 | Psychological Thriller | Urvashi, Joju George, Aishwarya Lekshmi

Dir: Safar Sanal. A fiercely protective mother of two daughters finds her life upended after a minor road accident with the volatile Jeemon spirals into a dangerous confrontation that tests her family and her sense of right and wrong. Urvashi delivers a commanding, layered performance — critics call her characterisation the best of the month. Available in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, and Hindi.

It's a Medical Miracle — ManoramaMAX | Oct 15 | Supernatural Comedy Thriller | Sangeeth Prathap, Sharafudheen

Dir: Shyamin Gireesh. A young man wakes after being declared brain-dead with an extraordinary sense of hearing. His search for the voice that kept him alive draws him into a web of masks, memories, and mystery. Inventive premise; first half is gripping though pacing slackens later. Also on Simply South. Available in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada.

Kappi Squad — JioHotstar | Oct 15 | Comedy-Drama Web Series

Created by Sreekanth Mohan; dir: Belraj Kalarikkal. Rinosh George leads a misfit gang of four emotionally unsettled young adults who turn insecurity and wounded pride into an unlikely café business. Found-family comedy; light, breezy, and youthful. Available in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, and Hindi.

Oru Anweshanathinte Thudakkam — Sun NXT | Oct 16 | Investigative Thriller

Dir: M. A. Nishad. Cast includes Shine Tom Chacko, Vani Viswanath, Sshivada, Swasika, Durga Krishna, Anumol, and Samuthirakani. A freelance journalist vanishes days after his engagement; the investigation that follows unfolds slowly with genuine tension. Best for fans of slow-burn mysteries. Malayalam only.

Adinaasham Vellapokkam — Saina Play | Oct 16 | Campus Comedy Thriller

Dir: A. J. Varghese. Shine Tom Chacko, Baiju Santhosh, Babu Antony, Manju Pillai. Four mischievous students at a notoriously strict engineering college have their prank routine derailed by a figure with a hidden agenda. Breezy, familiar campus comedy. Malayalam only.

🔶 Earlier October Malayalam OTT (Oct 1–14)

Khalifa — Prime Video | Oct 9 | Action Crime Thriller | Prithviraj Sukumaran ⭐

Dir: Vysakh. Prithviraj as Mambarakkal Aamir Ali — heir to a gold-smuggling empire drawn into cycles of rivalry, politics, and revenge. Commanding screen presence; Jakes Bejoy's score is pulsating. Also on Simply South, ManoramaMAX, Aha Video, and Lionsgate Play. Mohanlal cameo + sequel setup. Also available in Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Hindi.

Pradhama Drishtiya Kuttakkar — Netflix | Oct 9 | Police Crime Thriller | Parvathy Thiruvothu ⭐

Cast: Parvathy Thiruvothu, Mathew Thomas, Unnimaya Prasad, Sidharth Bharathan. Police officers caught in a dangerous situation after an unexpected incident at a police station — guilt, fear, and investigative pressure in a tightly controlled setting. Strong technical work. Available in Malayalam, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Hindi on Netflix.

Antappante Athbudha Pravarthikal — Prime Video | Oct 3 | Fantasy Comedy | IMDb 8.6 ⭐

An ordinary man's mundane life starts producing inexplicable, extraordinary events. Warm, inventive storytelling at its best — the most acclaimed Malayalam OTT original of the season. Malayalam only.

Bethlehem Kudumba Unit — JioHotstar | Oct 2 | Family Drama | IMDb 8.1 | #1 Trending ⭐

Dir: Girish AD. Nivin Pauly, Mamitha Baiju. Justin, an unmarried man, finds his routine upended when Ashley and her family move next door. Warm character-driven neighbourhood romance and family drama. Available in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Hindi.

Lurk — Sun NXT | Aug 26 | Action Thriller | Malayalam Only

Dir: M. A. Nishad. Saiju Kurup, Aju Varghese, Anumol, Manju Pillai. Set against Kerala's forest regions — recurring human-wildlife conflicts in a survival thriller that asks who the real predator is. Socially relevant. IMDb 5.2.

🗓 September Malayalam OTT — Full Rundown

Unmadham — SonyLIV | Sep 4 | Psychological Crime Thriller ⭐

Dir: Kiran Das. Kunchacko Boban, Lijomol Jose. A police constable burdened by his past reopens a long-forgotten case — supernatural elements begin to blur the line between reality and illusion. Gripped audiences with its moody atmospheric treatment. Available in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Hindi.

I, Nobody — JioHotstar | Aug 25 | Action Crime Thriller | Prithviraj Sukumaran ⭐

Dir: Nissam Basheer. Prithviraj Sukumaran, Parvathy Thiruvothu, Hakkim Shajahan. An ordinary government employee becomes connected to a serious crime and finds himself targeted by a system that refuses to believe him. Restrained and intense Prithviraj performance; Jakes Bejoy score. Available in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Hindi.

Thudakkam — JioHotstar | Sep 18 | Female-Led Action Thriller | Vismaya Mohanlal's Debut

Dir: Jude Anthany Joseph. Vismaya Mohanlal (Mohanlal's daughter in her acting debut), Aashish Joe Antony, Sai Kumar, Manoj K Jayan. A young martial arts practitioner becomes the target of a mysterious stalker. Female-led survival drama with commendable action work. Available in Malayalam, Telugu, Tamil, Hindi.

Magic Mushrooms — Prime Video | Sep 9 | Fantasy Comedy | Vishnu Unnikrishnan

Dir: Nadirshah. Vishnu Unnikrishnan, Akshaya Udhayakumar, Harisree Ashokan, Aju Varghese, Johny Antony, Jaffer Idukki. Mysterious mushrooms trigger unusual events in the village of Kanjikkuzhi — folklore, friendship, and the supernatural. Light family entertainer. Malayalam only (Prime Video).

Maharaja Hostel — Prime Video | Sep 11 | Horror Comedy

Dir: Charu Wakan. Akhil NRD, Akhil Shah, Sharath Babu, Sajin Cherukayil. College students experience spooky situations after moving into a hostel. Light horror-comedy for casual viewing. Available in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Hindi.

Achyuta Avataaram — Prime Video | Sep 11 | Cybercrime Drama | Malavika Nandan

Dir: Punith. A woman's life is shattered by a sophisticated cyber scam; she fights back through the digital underworld. Set in a traditional village in Kasaragod. Socially relevant cyber-fraud theme. Available in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Hindi.

Prince of Mollywood — JioHotstar | Sep 11 | Comedy-Drama Series | Dain Davis

An aspiring actor leaves his family business to pursue cinema — light, film-industry coming-of-age series. Available in 7 languages including Malayalam on JioHotstar.

Ananthan Kaadu — Prime Video | Sep 2 | Period Political Thriller | Arya, Murali Gopy

Dir: Jiyen Krishnakumar. Written by Murali Gopy. Set in 1990s Thiruvananthapuram — local thugs join forces with a Sri Lankan Tamil rebel. Politically charged period drama. Also on Simply South, Lionsgate Play, ManoramaMAX. Malayalam + Tamil.

Varavu — ZEE5 | Sep 11 | Action Thriller | Joju George, Murali Gopy, Arjun Ashokan

Dir: Shaji Kailas. Joju George as Kaattungal Paulson — a man returning home confronts buried crimes and corruption in a community marked by longstanding rivalries. Grounded and character-driven. IMDb 4.2. Malayalam only on ZEE5.

October's Big Hindi OTT Premieres

Gajab Raat — Netflix | Oct 23 | Hindi Crime Thriller

Director: Neeraj Pandey  |  Cast: Manoj Bajpayee, Divya Dutta, Nushrratt Bharuccha, Saqib Saleem, Akshay Oberoi, Kiku Sharda

Inspector Ajay Dikshit — a corrupt, money-hungry Delhi cop with plans for a very profitable night — has his scheme derailed when a badly injured girl is thrown from a moving car. Manoj Bajpayee plays his most morally ambiguous role in years.

Director: Jayant Rohatgi  |  Cast: Shriyam Bhagnani, Anubha Fatehpura, Nitesh Pandey

Inspired by the true story of Hetal Dave — India's pioneering female sumo wrestler. A completely uncharted subject for Indian cinema.

Jailer 2 — Full Story

Jailer 2 — Tamil + Hindi | Dir: Nelson Dilipkumar | Sun Pictures | Released Oct 15

Full Cast: Rajinikanth, Ramya Krishnan, Vidya Balan, SJ Suryah, Shiva Rajkumar, Mohanlal, Jackie Shroff, Suraj Venjaramoodu, Hrithik Roshan (special appearance) | Music: Anirudh Ravichander

After Tiger Muthuvel Pandian eliminates the Varman syndicate in Part 1, he relocates his family to remote Tamil Nadu and lives as a quiet supermarket supervisor. Varman's close aide Dileep (Suraj Venjaramoodu) is hunting for Varman's killer and three truckloads of hidden ancient statues. Nelson Dilipkumar goes bigger in every dimension — the film ends on Hrithik Roshan's entry explicitly promising Jailer 3. Rajinikanth at 75 doing things that will make you forget about age entirely.

Rated U/A 16+ | 14.15 lakh advance tickets on Day 1 | Vidya Balan in fierce villain mode

Drishyam The Conclusion — Full Story

Drishyam The Conclusion — Hindi | Oct 2 | Dir: Abhishek Pathak

Cast: Ajay Devgn, Shriya Saran, Ishita Dutta, Tabu

Years after covering up the accidental death that launched the franchise, Vijay Salgaonkar faces a new adversary — someone close enough to the original truth to destroy everything he has protected. Mohanlal, who played Vijay in the original Malayalam Drishyam, publicly praised the conclusion. Makers confirmed there will be no Drishyam 4.

₹507 crore worldwide | Second highest-grossing Indian film of 2026 | ₹240 crore opening week

India's Most Anticipated Film of the Decade — In Cinemas November 5, 2026

Ramayana – Part: I

Director: Nitesh Tiwari (Dangal, Chhichhore) | Music: Hans Zimmer + A.R. Rahman — first-ever collaboration on an Indian film

  • Lord Ram: Ranbir Kapoor
  • Ravana: Yash (KGF fame)

The Scale

  • Two-part epic — Part II arrives October 2027
  • Part II already 50% shot
  • International trailer unveiled Oct 16
  • Hans Zimmer + AR Rahman — unprecedented
  • Baahubali-level Hanuman reveal anticipation
  • OTT window: Jan–Feb 2027

The casting of Arun Govil — who played Lord Ram in the Doordarshan Ramayana that an entire generation grew up watching — as King Dashrath is a masterstroke of narrative poetry. The man who was Ram for India now plays Ram's father, passing the mantle on screen. Whether Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana soars or stumbles, nothing else in Indian cinema this decade will be as widely discussed.
