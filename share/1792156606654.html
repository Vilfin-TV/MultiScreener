OTT Update: September's Best Picks Still Streaming + October Arrivals + What's Coming in November 2026

October 16, 2026

Updated October 17, 2026 — comprehensive cross-checked guide covering September's must-watch carries, this week's live OTT drops, the theatrical events reshaping the box office, and every confirmed streaming date through November.

October's third week hits differently. Jailer 2 opened in cinemas yesterday — Rajinikanth returning as Tiger Muthuvel Pandian with a ferocity that had opening-day crowds on their feet, Vidya Balan in a scene-stealing role that has already overtaken the film's own lead in social chatter, and a Hrithik Roshan cameo that detonated Reddit twelve hours before the first show. Meanwhile, Drishyam The Conclusion crossed ₹507 crore globally in exactly two weeks — Ajay Devgn's franchise farewell is now the second highest-grossing Indian film of 2026 and is expected on JioHotstar within six weeks, meaning the most talked-about ending in Hindi cinema arrives on your sofa before Diwali. And looming on the horizon like a second sunrise: Ramayana – Part I opens in cinemas on November 5, nineteen days away, carrying the combined score of Hans Zimmer and A.R. Rahman, Ranbir Kapoor as Ram, Yash as Ravana, Sai Pallavi as Sita, and Sunny Deol as Hanuman waiting at the film's finale like Baahubali's "Why did Kattappa kill Baahubali?" — India's biggest theatrical gamble of the decade is almost here.

On streaming this week: Netflix's Malayalam psychological thriller Pradhama Drishtiya Kuttakkar is drawing strong early reactions, Prithviraj's action-drama Khalifa is live on Prime Video, and ZEE5's emotional family drama Aasha starring Urvashi dropped yesterday. Next Friday (Oct 23), Netflix drops Gajab Raat — Neeraj Pandey directing Manoj Bajpayee as a corrupt Delhi cop whose entire moral architecture collapses over a single catastrophic night. Everything below is verified from multiple sources, sorted by platform, and reviewed.

🔍 This Week's Big Stories

NETFLIX — MALAYALAM — OCT 16

Pradhama Drishtiya Kuttakkar

Genre: Psychological Thriller

A Malayalam psychological thriller built on a premise that refuses to let you settle — a detective investigating a series of identical crime scenes begins to suspect the killer has a face he recognises. Netflix's October Malayalam pick is drawing early comparisons to Drishyam for the deliberate way it withholds information. Recommended for dark-room watching.

PRIME VIDEO — MALAYALAM — OCT 16

Khalifa

Genre: Action Drama | Starring: Prithviraj Sukumaran

Prithviraj in full command of a role built around a reluctant enforcer navigating loyalty, territory, and the slow collapse of a criminal empire he never wanted to lead. The action is choreographed with restraint — every confrontation carries weight because you understand exactly what each character stands to lose. One of the better-constructed Malayalam action dramas of the year.

ZEE5 — MALAYALAM — OCT 16

Aasha

Genre: Family Drama | Starring: Urvashi

Urvashi carrying a family drama that leans entirely on emotional honesty rather than plot mechanics — a grandmother navigating a family pulling in three different directions after a single decision reshapes everyone's future. The kind of film Malayalam cinema makes better than anyone else, and ZEE5 has it exclusively from day one.

📅 Coming Next — Oct 23 Onwards

NETFLIX — HINDI — OCT 23

Gajab Raat ⭐ Most Anticipated of the Month

Genre: Crime Thriller | Director: Neeraj Pandey | Starring: Manoj Bajpayee

Neeraj Pandey — the man behind Special 26, Baby, and A Wednesday — returns with a single-night crime thriller that has been on Netflix India's most-anticipated list since June. Manoj Bajpayee plays a senior Delhi police officer whose entire career has been built on carefully managed corruption. In one night, every arrangement he has ever made starts to unravel simultaneously. Pandey's screenplay reportedly keeps five separate plot threads running in parallel before collapsing them into a single fifteen-minute sequence that the advance audience has been forbidden to discuss. Arrives October 23. Block the evening.

🏛️ THE BIG ONE — NOVEMBER 5

Ramayana – Part I

Theatrical Release | Pan-India | Director: Nitesh Tiwari

Ranbir Kapoor as Ram. Yash as Ravana. Sai Pallavi as Sita. Sunny Deol as Hanuman — held back until the film's final sequence in what sources are already calling the most structurally confident ending in modern Indian cinema. The score is a joint composition by Hans Zimmer and A.R. Rahman. The VFX pipeline reportedly ran over three years. India's biggest theatrical gamble of the decade opens November 5.

Every streaming number from September, every must-watch carry from early October — none of them have the event-cinema weight of this one. Drishyam The Conclusion had ₹507 crore and a nation holding its breath. Ramayana Part I has the entire mythology of a civilisation. Book your seats. The OTT window, whenever it arrives, will be a different experience entirely.
