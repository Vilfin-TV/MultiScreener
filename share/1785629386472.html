🎆 Tokyo Fireworks Festival 2026: Complete Ward-by-Ward Guide — All Dates, Routes & Map Links

August 2, 2026

Tokyo's beloved summer fireworks season — hanabi taikai (花火大会) — spans May through October, lighting up rivers, bays, and parks across every corner of the city. In 2026 over 15 major festivals are confirmed across Tokyo's wards and surrounding cities, the biggest season in years. Here is your complete ward-by-ward guide with exact dates, access routes, nearest stations, viewing spots, and Google Maps links.

🔴 HAPPENED TONIGHT — August 1, 2026: Three major fireworks festivals launched across Tokyo tonight. Edogawa (14,000 shells), Itabashi (15,000 shells), and Hachioji (4,000 shells) all fired up from 19:00.

💡 Essential Hanabi Viewing Tips

⏰ Arrive 2–3 Hours Early

Popular festivals fill fast. For Edogawa or Sumida-class events arrive by 17:00. Lay your blue tarp to claim your spot.

🎒 What to Bring

Tarp, insect repellent, portable fan (uchiwa), cold drinks, portable battery. Nearby convenience stores empty by 17:00.

🚃 Train Strategy

Post-festival trains are dangerously packed. Walk 1–2 stops from the main exit before boarding, or wait 30–60 min for crowds to thin.

👘 Wear a Yukata

Yukata rentals ¥3,000–6,000 in Asakusa and Harajuku. Fireworks + yukata = the quintessential Tokyo summer night.

🌧️ Rain Cancellations

Most festivals have a rain-backup date (usually next day). Always check the official website on the morning of the event.

💳 Load Your IC Card

Pre-load Suica or PASMO. QR and credit card systems at station gates can fail under extreme crowd loads on festival nights.
