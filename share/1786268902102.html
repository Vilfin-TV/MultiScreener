Malayalam Spotlight: Uyir (JioHotstar)

The only major Malayalam OTT release this week is Uyir, streaming on JioHotstar from August 4. Based on actual events, the story follows Ajeeb, a sub-inspector on probation at Dharmadam Police Station in Kannur district. It's a grounded, realistic crime drama that digs into the murky politics and moral compromises of police work in Kerala. If you're looking for your next Malayalam binge after Pallichattambi and Iruttu Ara, this is the one to put on.

Deep Dive: Operation Safed Sagar

This is the week's crown jewel. Operation Safed Sagar: The Highest Air Force Mission is Netflix India's most ambitious war drama since Rocket Boys. Set during the 1999 Kargil War, it follows the Golden Arrows squadron of the Indian Air Force on a dangerous mission behind Pakistani enemy lines. The show captures the nerve-shredding reality of high-altitude aerial combat — MiG-21s, missile locks, radio blackouts — without losing sight of the human story underneath. Filmed largely on location in Ladakh, this one is built for the big screen but lands on your phone. Don't watch it on mute.

Deep Dive: Vadhandhi Season 2

Vadhandhi Season 2: The Mystery of Mani is exactly what Tamil OTT fans have been waiting for since the first season's cliffhanger in 2022. Prime Video brought back the full creative team for this one. The new season follows a completely new case — the disappearance of a man named Mani from a small coastal town — with the same slow-burn, layered storytelling that made the original a hit. Watch Season 1 first if you haven't already: it's on Prime Video and takes about four hours.
