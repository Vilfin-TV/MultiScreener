Google Pixel 11 Series & Watch 5: Everything We Know Before the Big Reveal

August 9, 2026

The countdown has begun. On August 12, 2026 — just two days from now — Google will take the stage at the Made by Google event, hosted by comedian and TV presenter Trevor Noah, to officially unveil its biggest hardware lineup of the year. Phones. A new watch. And possibly Google's first-ever Bluetooth tracker.

The leaks have already told us almost everything. What they reveal is a lineup that is more evolution than revolution — except for one breathtaking thing hiding inside the chip.

The Chip That Changes Everything

Deep inside every Pixel 11 will beat the Tensor G6 — reportedly the world's first smartphone chip built on a 2nm manufacturing process. A "node" (the measure of how densely packed the transistors are inside a chip) this small means more performance, less battery drain, and faster AI — all at once. Apple's iPhone 18, arriving in September, was expected to hit 2nm. Google just beat them to it.

Google has also quietly dropped Samsung's Exynos modem in favour of a MediaTek M90 modem. That's significant: Samsung's modem has been widely criticised as the weak link in past Pixel connectivity. The M90 also brings satellite messaging — so you can send an SOS even where no signal exists.

A Camera That Glows — and Zooms Like a Telescope

Two big camera upgrades stand out. First: a new RGB LED ring on the rear camera visor, reportedly called Pixel Glow (or HiLight), brings notification lighting to the back of the phone. It glows. It blinks. No Pixel has done this before.

Second: zoom. The standard Pixel 11 reaches 30x digital zoom. The Pro models leap to a staggering 120x. That's territory previously dominated by Samsung's Space Zoom flagships. Google has built a rival — and on the world's most powerful mobile chip.

The Full Lineup

The Pixel 11 sports a 6.3-inch display at 1080 x 2424 pixels, arriving in four colours: Fuchsia, Light Sterling, Moss, and Midnight Haze. Storage starts at 256GB. The 128GB option is gone for good.

The Pixel 11 Pro shares the same screen size but brings the full 120x zoom camera and Gemini Intelligence. RAM drops slightly from 16GB to 12GB compared to last year — though in practice, most users won't notice.

The Pixel 11 Pro XL steps up to a 6.8-inch display and a huge 5,115mAh battery — one of the largest Google has ever shipped in a Pixel phone.

The Pixel 11 Pro Fold offers an 8-inch inner foldable display and a 4,800mAh battery, trimmed slightly from the previous generation's 5,015mAh — likely shaved for a slimmer form factor.

Every device in the lineup runs Gemini Intelligence — Google's AI capable of multi-step tasks, visual context understanding, and acting across your apps like a genuine personal assistant.

Pixel Watch 5: Style as a Statement

The Pixel Watch 5 arrives in 41mm and 45mm sizes, with Wi-Fi and LTE options. Under the hood, it runs the same Snapdragon Wear W5 Gen 2 chip as last year's Watch 4 — the hardware story here is modest. But there are real additions: better sleep tracking, pulse detection alerts, and Gemini Intelligence on your wrist.

Google's own teaser video for the Watch 5, released August 7, said it all with one line. While Apple keeps its rectangular watch and Samsung has moved to a squircle shape, Google's Watch 5 "still has the decency to look like a watch." A quiet but pointed dig at both rivals.

Prices start at $399 for the 41mm Wi-Fi — a $50 jump over the Watch 4.

One More Thing: Pixel Tag

Tucked in alongside everything else is the Pixel Tag — Google's answer to Apple's AirTag. Priced at just €35 in Europe, it uses ultra-wideband (UWB) technology to show you the precise distance and direction to a lost item. Small, cheap, and potentially the sleeper hit of the whole event.

What This All Means

Pixel 11 is not a radical reinvention. The design barely changes. The Watch gets the same chip as last year. And prices are going up — there's no softening that.

But look under the surface. The Tensor G6 is a genuine world first: the only 2nm chip shipping in a smartphone right now, ahead of even Apple's iPhone 18. The 120x Pro zoom puts Google's camera in telescope territory. And Gemini Intelligence isn't a chatbot — it's an AI that acts, not just answers.

Google's bet with the Pixel 11 is clear: the future of the smartphone is won in silicon and software, not in slimmer bezels. Whether buyers pay the higher price to join that bet — we find out in two days.
