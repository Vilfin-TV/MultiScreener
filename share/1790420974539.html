OTT Week 4 September 2026: Toxic, Shaque, UNABOMBER, Brothers & 25+ Streams This Week

September 26, 2026

Updated September 26, 2026 — complete, cross-checked guide to every major OTT drop this week.

September's final full streaming week is built around two words: finally and wait, what? Toxic, Yash's ₹500-crore Kannada gangster epic that divided critics and disappointed box offices, finally lands on ZEE5 — giving millions the chance to form their own verdict on Geetu Mohandas' bold, operatic misfire. On the same Friday, Netflix drops Shaque: Trust No One, Parineeti Chopra hunting the abductor of her newborn across nine years of obsession, alongside the year's most unexpected double act: Russell Crowe as the Harvard psychologist who may have helped create the Unabomber. Meanwhile, Apple TV+ delivers its most self-aware concept of 2026 — Matthew McConaughey and Woody Harrelson playing themselves as men who discover they might literally be brothers. And JioHotstar's Hunkkaar: The Roar tackles a girl's assault allegation against a powerful cult leader with a ferocity that has already made it the most-discussed Indian series launch of the month. Twenty-five-plus titles across six languages. Here is everything, verified and sorted.

The Big Stories This Week

ZEE5 — KANNADA / ALL LANGUAGES — SEP 26

Toxic: A Fairy Tale for Grown-Ups — Yash, Kiara Advani, Nayanthara

Director: Geetu Mohandas  |  Runtime: 192 min  |  RT: 21% critics / 4.6 avg  |  Box office: ₹341 cr vs ~₹500 cr budget

The most anticipated Kannada film in years limped out of theatres after underperforming against its enormous budget — and now arrives on ZEE5 for the streaming verdict. Set in Portuguese-ruled Goa post-Independence, Toxic follows the rise and catastrophic unravelling of Raya, a gangster across decades of violence, with a third-act twist that critics called either brave or baffling: Raya's supposed son Ticket is revealed as a dissociated alter ego, the real Ticket having died years ago. The film is really about toxic masculinity as a self-consuming myth.

What critics agreed on: Yash and Kiara Advani's performances are genuinely committed, the visual scale is staggering, and a few set pieces are legitimately stunning. Where it collapsed: the screenplay is too thin for its 192-minute runtime, the symbolism is heavy-handed, and the film's ambition constantly outruns its execution. Rotten Tomatoes has it at just 21%, with the critical consensus landing around "visually impressive but hollowed out." Audiences who went in expecting a Pushpa-style crowd thriller were especially disappointed.

The streaming version is the right place to judge it honestly. At home, at your own pace, the film's genuine visual accomplishments and committed central performances have more room to breathe. The flaws remain. But Toxic is not the disaster it was made out to be by a section of critics — it is a genuinely ambitious swing that misses its centre. Also available dubbed in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam.

NETFLIX — HINDI SERIES — SEP 24

Shaque: Trust No One — Parineeti Chopra, Jennifer Winget, Tahir Raj Bhasin

Director: Rensil D'Silva  |  8 Episodes  |  Crime / Mystery Thriller

A mother receives the first photograph of her stolen newborn nine years after the abduction. That single image — and the question of who sent it and why — unravels everyone she trusted. Director Rensil D'Silva, who made Karthik Calling Karthik and co-wrote Rang De Basanti, co-creates here with Siddharth P. Malhotra in what looks like the year's most psychologically complex Hindi thriller.

Parineeti Chopra has been building to a role like this for years. Jennifer Winget, Tahir Raj Bhasin, Soni Razdan, Sumeet Vyas and Harleen Sethi complete an ensemble cast where almost nobody is who they appear to be. The thriller structure — who can be trusted, who sent the photo, what happened that night nine years ago — runs across eight episodes. Early screening reports call it "relentlessly gripping" and "the kind of show that makes you cancel plans." Netflix's strongest Hindi series launch in months.

JIOHOTSTAR — HINDI SERIES — SEP 25

Hunkkaar: The Roar — Fatima Sana Shaikh, Mohammed Zeeshan Ayyub, Aneet Padda

Directors: Nitya Mehra, Karan Kapadia  |  8 Episodes  |  Crime / Courtroom Drama

A teenage girl accuses a revered and powerful spiritual cult leader of sexual assault. One allegation. The entire system — police, courts, media, devotees — mobilises against her. Hunkkaar: The Roar is a courtroom and social thriller about how institutions fail women, and it arrives at a moment when India's #MeToo accountability conversations are resurfacing in the media cycle.

Fatima Sana Shaikh plays a lawyer who takes the girl's case. Mohammed Zeeshan Ayyub plays the cult leader — a role that reportedly required months of preparation and has already drawn comparisons to Manoj Bajpayee at his most controlled. Raghubir Yadav, Ram Kapoor and Arjun Mathur complete a cast of heavyweights. Directors Nitya Mehra and Karan Kapadia bring a visual austerity that suits the subject. The show is being described as JioHotstar's most important original since Scam 1992 in terms of ambition and social resonance. Do not sleep on this one.

NETFLIX — ENGLISH FILM — SEP 25

UNABOMBER — Russell Crowe, Jacob Tremblay, Shailene Woodley

Director: Janus Metz Pedersen  |  Writers: Sam Chalsen, Nelson Greaves  |  Biographical Thriller

The film that critics are calling "the most thrilling Netflix original in years" makes one central, disturbing argument: the Harvard psychologist who subjected young Ted Kaczynski to months of dehumanising psychological experiments in the 1950s helped, perhaps directly, to create the Unabomber. Jacob Tremblay plays the young Kaczynski at Harvard. Russell Crowe plays Dr. Henry Murray — charming, brilliant, sadistic. Shailene Woodley plays the FBI agent hunting the Unabomber decades later as his package bombs kill across America.

The dual-timeline structure gives director Janus Metz Pedersen the architecture to build an essay on how institutions manufacture violence. Crowe in particular is reported to be giving one of the best performances of his career — a villain who believes he is helping science while destroying a mind. A genuinely important film about America's relationship with institutional cruelty.

APPLE TV+ — ENGLISH SERIES — SEP 23

Brothers — Matthew McConaughey & Woody Harrelson

Creators: McConaughey, Harrelson, David West Read, Lee Eisenberg  |  8 Episodes  |  Comedy  |  RT: 57%

McConaughey and Harrelson play exaggerated fictional versions of themselves — two movie stars living as neighbours on McConaughey's Texas ranch after Harrelson's daughter's wedding falls apart. Then a DNA test suggests they might genuinely be biological brothers. The premise mirrors real-life: both actors have publicly discussed DNA results showing shared ancestry. Holland Taylor, Natalie Martinez, and Dax Shepard round out the cast.

Critics are split at 57% RT — some find it warm and genuinely moving in its bromance chemistry, others find it self-indulgent. What nobody disputes: the McConaughey-Harrelson chemistry is effortless and real. Two episodes drop September 23, weekly through November 4.
