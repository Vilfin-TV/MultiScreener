🌍Stop Losing Money to Hidden Bank Fees! The Ultimate Showdown: Revolut vs. Instarem vs. Wise

June 17, 2026

Welcome back to Vilfin TV!

If you are sending money across borders, you already know the pain of watching traditional banks take a massive bite out of your hard-earned cash through poor exchange rates and sneaky hidden fees.

The good news? The era of traditional banking monopolies is over. Today, we are looking at the heavyweights of international money transfers: Revolut, Instarem, and Wise. We are breaking down all their features, pros, and cons to crown the ultimate champion for your wallet.

Let's dive into the ultimate comparison!

🌍 Revolut: The All-In-One Financial Super App

Revolut has evolved far beyond a simple transfer service into a massive global neobank. If you want a platform that handles everything from international transfers to your daily coffee purchases, Revolut is a powerhouse.

Standout Features

Revolut offers a complete digital banking experience. You can hold and exchange over 70 currencies, open joint accounts, and even dive into investments. Their multi-currency debit card is a traveler's best friend, allowing you to spend abroad with zero friction. Premium tiers even unlock perks like airport lounge access and travel insurance.

🎁 Beyond Transfers: The "Extra Perks" Showdown

When you sign up for these apps, you aren't just getting a way to send money—you are unlocking a whole ecosystem of financial tools. Here is how they stack up when it comes to extra features like debit cards, buying gold, and travel eSIMs:

🌍 Revolut: The King of Extras

If you love having every financial tool in one place, Revolut is unmatched. They constantly add new features to make travel and investing easier.

  • Debit Card: Yes! You get a sleek physical and virtual debit card for fee-free spending in over 150 currencies.
  • Buy Gold & Commodities: Yes! You can invest in Gold, Silver, and even crypto directly from the app with just a few taps.
  • Travel eSIMs: Yes! Revolut recently launched in-app eSIMs. You can buy digital data plans for over 100 countries directly from the app, so you never have to hunt for a local SIM card when you land.
  • Other Perks: Premium tiers unlock airport lounge access, travel insurance, and advanced budgeting tools.

The Pros

  • Insane Versatility: It is not just for transfers; it is a full daily banking replacement.
  • Travel Ready: The multi-currency card makes spending abroad seamless and cheap.
  • Instant Transfers: Sending money to other Revolut users is instant and free globally.

The Cons

  • Weekend Markups: They charge an extra fee (around 1%) if you exchange currency over the weekend when the global markets are closed.
  • Tiered Limits: The free standard plan has limits on fee-free currency exchange and ATM withdrawals before surcharges kick in.

⚡ Instarem: The Fast & Rewarding Challenger

Instarem might be the dark horse in this race, but it is an absolute game-changer for regular senders. Designed purely to make overseas transfers fast and cheap, it brings something highly unique to the table: a loyalty rewards program.

Standout Features

Instarem covers over 60 countries and focuses on getting your money there faster and cheaper than most traditional methods. What truly sets them apart is their InstaPoints system. Every time you transfer money, you earn points that you can redeem for discounts on future transfers. It literally pays to keep using them.

The Pros

  • Reward System: Earning InstaPoints on transfers means your fees get cheaper over time.
  • Extremely Competitive Rates: They offer razor-thin margins on exchange rates, especially for Asian corridors.
  • Speed: Most transfers arrive on the same day, and many are practically instant.

The Cons

  • Fewer Extra Features: It lacks the broad daily banking tools (like savings vaults) that Revolut offers.
  • No Multi-Currency Holding: You are primarily just sending money from point A to point B, rather than holding balances in a digital wallet.

⚡ Instarem: The Focused Sender (With a Travel Bonus)

Instarem keeps things lean, prioritizing cheap transfers and reward points, but they are starting to add some clever travel features.

  • Debit Card: Regional. Instarem offers the highly popular "Amaze" card, which lets you link existing bank cards to avoid foreign transaction fees, but it is currently only available in specific regions like Singapore.
  • Buy Gold: No. Instarem does not offer wealth management, crypto, or commodity trading.
  • Travel eSIMs: Yes! Instarem recently added travel eSIMs directly in their app. You can check availability and buy flexible data plans for your travel destinations right alongside your money transfers.
  • Other Perks: The InstaPoints rewards system remains their biggest extra, giving you cashback-style discounts on your transfers

💚 Wise (Formerly TransferWise): The King of Transparency

Wise revolutionized the financial industry by exposing hidden bank markups, and they remain the gold standard for honest pricing. They do one thing incredibly well: converting and moving money at the exact mid-market exchange rate.

Standout Features

Wise gives you the "Google exchange rate" — the real, mid-market rate with absolutely zero markup. They charge a transparent, upfront percentage fee so you know exactly what you are paying down to the cent. Their borderless account allows you to hold over 40 currencies and gives you local bank details for countries around the world.

The Clean & Simple Traveler

Wise focuses strictly on moving and managing money efficiently without cluttering the app with speculative investments.

  • Debit Card: Yes! The Wise debit card is legendary for travelers. It auto-converts your balance to the local currency at the mid-market rate with tiny fees. You also get virtual cards for safe online shopping.
  • Buy Gold: No. Wise does not offer commodities or crypto. However, depending on your region, they offer a feature called "Assets" where your balance can earn interest by being held in government backed funds.
  • Travel eSIMs: No. Wise focuses purely on financial tools, so you will still need to buy your travel data elsewhere.
  • Other Perks: The ability to get local bank account details (like routing and account numbers) in over 10 currencies so you can receive money like a local.

The Pros

  • Zero Exchange Rate Markup: You always get the real mid-market rate. No hidden margins, ever.
  • Local Bank Details: You can receive money like a local in multiple regions (US, UK, EU, Australia, etc.).
  • Clear Pricing: Their fee calculator is incredibly transparent; what you see is exactly what arrives.

The Cons

  • Fees on Everything: While completely transparent, they do charge a small fee for every single transfer (no "fee-free" allowances like Revolut).
  • Transfer Speeds Vary: While often instant, some less common currency routes can take a day or two depending on local banking hours.

🏆 The Verdict: Which One is the Best?

So, which app takes the crown? The truth is, the "best" app depends entirely on how you use your money:

  • Choose Wise if your main priority is transparency, sending large amounts of money, or needing to receive money from different countries like a local.
  • Choose Revolut if you want a complete, all-in-one digital bank. If you travel frequently, want a sleek card for everyday spending, and love sending instant cash to friends, Revolut is unmatched.
  • Choose Instarem if you make frequent, recurring remittances. The InstaPoints reward system makes it the absolute most cost-effective choice for loyal users who send money home regularly.
