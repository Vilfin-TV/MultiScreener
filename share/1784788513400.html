Apple’s iPhone 18 Series: Leaks Reveal 2nm A20 Chip, Variable Aperture Camera, and Under-Display Face ID Upgrade

July 23, 2026

Apple is gearing up for one of its most substantial hardware shifts in years with the upcoming iPhone 18 series. Following reports surrounding the iPhone 17 family, industry supply chain leaks indicate that the iPhone 18 lineup will introduce cutting-edge semiconductor manufacturing, major camera hardware overhauls, and upgraded display architectures designed to supercharge Apple Intelligence.

TSMC 2nm Process and WMCM Architecture

At the core of the iPhone 18 Pro models will be Apple’s next-generation A20 chip, manufactured using TSMC’s state-of-the-art 2-nanometer (2nm) process node. Beyond the raw node shrink, Apple is expected to transition to Wafer-Level Multi-Chip Module (WMCM) packaging. This allows the CPU, GPU, Neural Engine, and RAM to be integrated more tightly, drastically boosting memory bandwidth and power efficiency for complex local AI computations.

Variable Aperture Main Camera

Camera enthusiasts have a major upgrade to anticipate. Leaks suggest that upper-tier iPhone 18 models will feature a physical variable aperture primary lens. This hardware milestone will allow the lens to dynamically adjust light intake, enabling true optical depth-of-field control and significantly superior low-light performance without relying solely on software synthesis.

Under-Display Face ID Integration

Apple is continuing its push toward a seamless glass slab design. The iPhone 18 series is rumored to introduce under-display Face ID technology. By housing the primary infrared sensors beneath the OLED panel, Apple can significantly shrink the Dynamic Island footprint down to a single punch-hole cutout for the selfie camera.

12GB RAM Standard Across Flagship Lineup

To sustain advanced Apple Intelligence models and multi-tasking workflows locally on-device, the iPhone 18 series is slated to equip flagships with 12GB of high-speed LPDDR5X RAM, ensuring seamless execution of next-gen generative AI tasks.

Custom In-House 5G Modem

Building upon initial deployments in intermediate models, Apple is aiming to integrate its proprietary 5G modem silicon into the iPhone 18 lineup, reducing reliance on third-party modems and delivering tailored power optimizations for cellular connectivity.

Expected Announcement and Availability

Following Apple's traditional release cycle, the iPhone 18 lineup is anticipated to officially launch in September 2026, setting new benchmarks for premium smartphones worldwide.
