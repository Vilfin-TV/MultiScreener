🇮🇳 Edelweiss Launches India's First REITs Index Fund: All You Need to Know! 🏠

July 25, 2026

Edelweiss Mutual Fund has launched India's first REIT (Real Estate Investment Trust) based index fund. Named the Edelweiss Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund, this new scheme presents a great opportunity for those looking to invest in the real estate sector.

What are REITs? 🤔

Simply put, a REIT is a system that distributes rental income from large commercial complexes, shopping malls, and office buildings to investors. Instead of buying buildings directly, it allows you to participate in such large projects with a small investment amount.

Where does this fund invest? 🏢

This fund primarily invests in the 15 leading companies and REITs included in the Nifty REITs & Realty Index. This includes operators of large commercial complexes in India such as Embassy Office Parks REIT, Mindspace Business Parks REIT, and Nexus Select Trust. Additionally, it invests in the shares of top real estate developers like DLF, Godrej Properties, Macrotech Developers (Lodha), Phoenix Mills, Prestige Estates, and Sobha. This means that through a single fund, we can participate in some of the best real estate projects in India.

Opportunities and Risks 📊

These funds help in gaining both regular rental income (Dividend Yield) and appreciation of property value (Capital Appreciation). However, changes in bank interest rates and fluctuations in the real estate market may affect its performance. For those willing to invest for the long term, this is an excellent option to diversify their portfolio.

Key features of this fund:

  • Triple Income Potential: Investors can potentially earn returns in three ways: regular dividends from rental yields, interest income, and capital appreciation from the increase in property and stock values over time.
  • Dual Benefits: This fund invests not only in REITs but also in the stocks of prominent real estate developers.
  • Low Investment: You can start investing in this fund with just ₹100.
  • Reliability: The fund tracks the Nifty REITs & Realty Index.
  • Tax Benefits: Due to its mutual fund structure, it may offer better tax benefits compared to buying REITs directly.

Important note for investors:

The subscription for this fund begins on August 5 and remains open until August 19. Another major advantage is that there is no exit load.
