Zerodha Opens US Stock Investing From Today: A New Gateway for Indian Investors to Buy Global Equities

October 21, 2026

Indian retail investors now have an easier pathway to diversify their portfolios internationally. Starting today, brokerage giant Zerodha has officially opened up US stock investing, allowing users to seamlessly buy and invest in global giants like Apple, Microsoft, Tesla, Google, and Nvidia directly from India.

What This Means for Indian Investors

Investing in overseas markets has traditionally involved complex paperwork, high foreign exchange transfer fees, and cumbersome compliance with the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS). With Zerodha's integration, the process is streamlined, offering a transparent, cost-effective, and user-friendly gateway to global equities.

Key Highlights of Zerodha’s US Investing Feature

  • Direct Access to US Markets: Buy fractional or full shares of top-tier US listed companies and Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs).
  • Seamless LRS Integration: Easy compliance and hassle-free remittance procedures aligned with RBI guidelines.
  • Competitive Pricing: Low brokerage and transparent currency conversion charges compared to traditional wire transfers.
  • Portfolio Diversification: Hedge currency risk and build exposure to dollar-denominated assets.

How to Get Started

To begin investing in US stocks through Zerodha, users can log into their trading account, complete the necessary international investing activation steps under LRS guidelines, fund their international wallet, and start trading.
