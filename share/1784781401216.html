🆕 Faster option — Japan eVisa: Since 2024, eligible Indian tourists can apply for a single-entry, up to 90-day tourist eVisa entirely online — no VFS appointment, no biometrics visit, no passport sticker. Applies through MOFA-accredited agencies; check current eligibility and apply at the Embassy of Japan in India's official eVisa page. The traditional VFS/BLS sticker-visa route below still works too, and remains necessary for multiple-entry visas.

India Exit Immigration

1. Reach airport 3 hours before international flight

2. Check-in → Security → Immigration

3. ECR check: If your passport has ECR stamp and going for work — emigration clearance needed. For TOURISM, NOT required

4. Show passport + boarding pass + return ticket

5. Boarding gate

Japan Entry Immigration

1. Visit Japan Web — fill online BEFORE travel at Visit Japan Web (vjw-lp.digital.go.jp). Creates QR codes for immigration + customs. Saves 20-30 minutes!

2. Immigration counter — fingerprint + photo + passport stamp ("Temporary Visitor")

3. Baggage claim

4. Customs — QR or paper form

5. Green channel = nothing to declare | Red channel = items over ¥1L

Duty-Free Limits:

- Alcohol: 3 bottles (760ml each)

- Tobacco: 200 cigarettes / 50 cigars / 250g tobacco

- Perfume: 2oz

- Other goods: Up to ¥2,00,000
