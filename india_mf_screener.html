Before you invest in an NFO
  • A ₹10 offer price does not make a fund cheaper — returns depend on what the fund holds, not its unit price.
  • A new fund has no performance record. Compare it with established funds in the same category in Explore.
  • Index funds and ETFs that track an index already offered by other funds add little — prefer the lowest cost and tightest tracking.
  • ETFs trade on the stock exchange after listing and need a demat account; regular mutual fund units do not.
  • Check the exit load, stated asset allocation and riskometer in the scheme documents before applying.
  • Most open-ended funds re-open for regular purchases and SIPs within a few days of allotment — there is rarely a need to rush.

How to read this screener
  • Returns up to 1 year are absolute; 3, 5 and 10 year returns are annualised (CAGR). Since-inception returns start from the launch of the Direct plan.
  • SIP returns assume ₹10,000 invested on the same date every month and are annualised (XIRR).
  • Rolling returns show every possible holding period — the best, the worst and how often the fund beat its benchmark. They reveal consistency that point-to-point returns hide.
  • Risk ratios use 36 monthly returns to the last month-end. Higher Sharpe / Sortino means more return per unit of risk; a downside capture below 100 means the fund fell less than its benchmark in falling months.
  • Factor DNA shows which investment style best explains an equity fund's behaviour — Momentum, Value, Quality or Low Volatility — together with its market-cap tilt.
  • VilfinTV Rating ranks funds within their category on risk-adjusted return, downside protection, long-term return and cost. Funds need a 3-year record to be rated. Sectoral and thematic funds are grouped by theme (banking, pharma, technology, infrastructure and more) and rated against all sectoral / thematic funds.
Methodology & FAQ
Which funds are covered?

Every open-ended Indian mutual fund scheme in its Direct plan, Growth option — equity, debt, hybrid, commodity, international, index and solution-oriented funds. ETFs are covered separately in ETF Momentum Mode.

How are categories assigned?

Funds follow their official SEBI category. Fund-of-funds and index funds are further grouped by what they actually hold: gold and silver funds under Commodity, overseas funds under Global, debt index and target-maturity funds under Debt, and passive equity funds under Index.

When is the data updated?

Every morning, once fund houses have published the previous business day's NAV. Risk ratios, rolling returns and Factor DNA are computed as of the last month-end, the convention used in fund factsheets.

Which benchmark is used?

Equity funds are compared with the index matching their category (Nifty 100 for large caps, Nifty Midcap 150, Nifty Smallcap 250, Nifty LargeMidcap 250, otherwise Nifty 500). Benchmark returns include dividends: each index is tracked through its longest-running Direct-plan index fund, so the comparison is like-for-like after costs. Hybrid funds show equity sensitivity against the Nifty 50. Debt, commodity and international funds are compared with their category peers.

How is the risk-free rate chosen?

Sharpe and Sortino ratios subtract the overnight money-market return for the same months, so they adapt automatically as interest rates change.

What do the scores and status mean?

Consistency is the share of the last 36 month-ends in which the fund ranked in the top half of its category on rolling 1-year return. Recency blends 3-month, 6-month and 1-year returns (20/30/50) into a category percentile. Volatility ranks 3-year volatility within the category (100 = calmest). Status: In form — recency in the top 30%; On track — recency between the 40th and 70th percentile; Underperforming — recency in the bottom 40% but a top-half long-term record; Out of track — both recent and long-term returns in the bottom half. Index funds are not scored, since they track an index by design.

How is taxation shown?

Each fund is mapped to its capital-gains treatment from its SEBI category and stated asset allocation: equity-oriented, specified (debt-oriented), or other. Rates exclude surcharge and cess and may change with each Union Budget — please confirm with a tax professional.

Where do portfolio holdings come from?

From each fund house's statutory monthly portfolio disclosure, published within ten days of every month-end. The Portfolio tab shows the top holdings, sector allocation, asset mix and market-cap split as of that month-end. Market-cap buckets follow SEBI's ranking — Nifty 100 constituents are large cap, Nifty Midcap 150 constituents are mid cap and other listed stocks are small cap. Portfolios are being added fund house by fund house.

How does the Smart SIP planner work?

Choose your investment horizon and risk level, and the planner builds a diversified plan of up to eight funds across equity, hybrid, debt and gold — with small thematic and international satellites only for aggressive plans of seven years or more. The Very aggressive level always combines equity, debt and gold or silver. Its core is an actively managed large & mid cap fund instead of a Nifty 50 index fund (3+ years). It holds more mid and small caps, and adds a value / contrarian fund (5–10 years), a rules-based momentum index fund (10+ years) and a medium-term corporate-bond or medium-duration debt fund. That spreads a growth-heavy plan across market caps, investing styles and asset classes. Funds must have a long record, beat their benchmark in most rolling periods, cost no more than their category median and hold low overlap with each other. Plans are reviewed monthly and stay stable: a fund is replaced only after failing two reviews in a row, and the number of funds never grows. A small market-condition tilt adds equity when prices are well below recent highs and trims it when valuations look stretched.

What does “Fresh investment paused” mean?

Some funds — mostly overseas funds when the industry-wide overseas investment limit is reached — temporarily stop accepting new lump-sum purchases or SIPs. Existing investments are unaffected. Use the purchase-status filter in Explore to see which global funds are open for investment today.

Where do new fund offers come from?

The NFO tab lists schemes in their official new-fund-offer period, with offer dates, minimum investment, category and links to the offer document and fund house. Recently closed offers stay listed for 45 days.

How is Factor DNA calculated?

Three years of weekly returns are matched against NSE's Momentum, Value, Quality, Low-Volatility and size indices. The best-fitting combination (weights that are positive and add to 100%) shows the fund's style. A fund is labelled with a single style only when that style dominates and the fit is strong; otherwise it is a Blend.
